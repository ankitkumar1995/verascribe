# Cedar Workshop demo handbook

This is fictional sample data for testing VeraScribe. It does not describe a real organization.

## Opening hours

Cedar Workshop opens Tuesday through Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00. It is closed on Sunday and Monday.

## Membership

A standard membership costs 25 credits per month. Membership includes access to the reading room and two tool-training sessions each month. Tool-training sessions must be booked at least 24 hours in advance.

## Borrowing tools

Members may borrow up to three hand tools at a time for seven days. Power tools remain inside the workshop. Tools must be returned to the front desk during opening hours.

## Cancellation

A tool-training booking can be cancelled without penalty until 12 hours before the session. Later cancellations use one of the member's monthly training sessions.

## Contact

Questions about memberships can be taken to the front desk. This handbook does not specify a phone number, email address, refund policy, or parking arrangements.
